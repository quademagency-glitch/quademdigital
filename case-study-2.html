Scaling User Acquisition with Targeted Ads

Implemented a multi-channel ad strategy to bring in high-quality leads.

Cover Image 2

The Challenge

A promising B2B SaaS startup was struggling to acquire users efficiently. Their customer acquisition cost (CAC) was too high, and their existing ad campaigns were bringing in low-quality leads that didn't convert to paid subscriptions.

Our Approach

We revamped their entire digital marketing strategy. First, we clarified their ideal customer profile (ICP). Then, we launched highly targeted campaigns across LinkedIn and Google Ads, focusing on intent-based keywords and personalized ad copy. We also implemented robust retargeting funnels.

The Results

50% Reduction in CPA

The new strategy yielded immediate results. Within two months, the cost per acquisition (CPA) was cut in half, while the volume of highly qualified leads increased by over 200%. This enabled the startup to hit their growth targets ahead of schedule.

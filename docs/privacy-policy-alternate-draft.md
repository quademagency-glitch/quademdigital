# Privacy policy: an alternate draft, not the live page

Written by a parallel session on 27 August 2026 as a hardcoded route at
src/pages/privacy-policy/index.astro. That route was removed on 29 August 2026
and never deployed, and this file is the copy preserved so the writing is not
lost.

**Why it was removed.** The live privacy policy is a Payload `pages` document
and has been since 24 August. Astro sorts static route segments ahead of
dynamic ones, so this file would have served /privacy-policy/ and the CMS
document would never have rendered. That would have silently dropped four
sections the live policy carries and this draft does not:

- "Businesses we contact who have not contacted us", which the outbound
  campaign legally depends on and which was added to the CMS on 29 August
- Artificial Intelligence
- Cookies and Tracking
- Where Your Information Is Stored

The page would still have answered 200, so nothing would have reported it.

**What is worth taking from it.** The register is better than the live policy's
in places, and it is closer to how /privacy/outreach reads. Three things worth
porting into the CMS document by hand:

- "The short version", a plain summary before the detail
- "What I never do", which states the negative commitments explicitly
- "How to ask", which tells a reader the actual steps rather than naming rights

Porting them is a copy decision on a legal page, so it is left for Ernest
rather than done here.

Privacy policy

            

                What I collect, why I collect it, who else touches it, and how to make me
                delete it. Written to be read rather than to cover me.
            

            
Who I am

            

                I am Ernest Avorwlanu. I run Quadem Digital, a small studio in Ghana. I am the
                person responsible for the information described here, and the person who
                answers when you write about it.
            

            
The short version

            

                I collect the least I can get away with, I do not sell it, I do not share it
                with advertisers, and if you ask me to delete it I will and I will tell you
                when it is done.
            

            
What I collect, and when

            
If you just visit the website

            

                The site runs Google Analytics. It records things like which pages were
                viewed, roughly where in the world the visit came from, and what kind of
                device it was. It sets cookies to do that. I use it to see which pages are
                worth keeping. I do not know who you are from it.
            

            
If you fill in a form or subscribe

            

                Your name, email address, and whatever you typed. Email is sent through
                Resend. If you subscribed to anything, every message carries an unsubscribe
                link and it works immediately.
            

            
If you message the Quadem WhatsApp number

            

                Your WhatsApp number, the name shown on your WhatsApp profile, and the
                messages you send. The intake system asks three questions and stores the
                answers: what you are looking for, which city you are in, and whether you
                already have a website along with its address. That is the whole of it.
            

            

                Replies you receive within a conversation may be sent automatically. Once the
                three answers are captured, the automation stops and I read the conversation
                myself.
            

            
If I emailed you and you never asked me to

            

                Your business name, a business email address, and notes about your website
                taken from looking at it. All from public sources. There is a fuller
                explanation on 
why you got an email from us
.
            

            
If you become a client

            

                Whatever the work requires: contact details, billing details, and access to
                the systems I am being paid to build or fix. I keep credentials only for as
                long as the work needs them and I ask you to rotate them when it ends.
            

            
Who else touches it

            

                I do not do all of this myself. These are the companies that process
                information on my behalf, and nothing goes to anyone else:
            

            

                

Google
: website analytics.

                

Meta (WhatsApp)
: carries WhatsApp messages between you and me.

                

Supabase
: the database where enquiries are stored. Servers in Europe.

                

Resend
: sends email.

                

The site host
: serves these pages and keeps standard server logs.

            

            

                Because of that, information about you may be stored outside Ghana, mainly in
                Europe and the United States. It is held under the same terms wherever it sits.
            

            
What I never do

            

                I do not sell your information. I do not rent or trade it. I do not hand it to
                advertisers or data brokers. I do not use what you tell me in a WhatsApp
                enquiry to target you with advertising anywhere.
            

            
How long I keep it

            

                Enquiries that go nowhere: about a year, then deleted. Client records: as long
                as the work runs, plus the period Ghanaian tax and accounting rules require me
                to keep invoices. Analytics: on Google's own retention schedule. If you ask me
                to delete something sooner, I will, unless the law requires me to keep it, in
                which case I will tell you which part and why.
            

            
Your rights

            

                Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) you can ask me what I hold
                about you, ask me to correct it, ask me to delete it, or object to my holding
                it at all. If you are in the UK or the European Union, you have the equivalent
                rights there and I will honour them the same way.
            

            

                You do not need a reason and you do not need to be a client. Ask and I will
                answer within a month, usually the same week.

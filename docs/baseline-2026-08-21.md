## AI visibility

**Not measured, and here is why.**

The Ahrefs account is connected but every endpoint returns "Insufficient plan",
including Brand Radar, which is the tool that measures AI mentions and citations.
An earlier draft of `analysis-pipeline.md` assumed Brand Radar was available. It
is not, on this plan.

Semrush does expose `serp_ai_overview_keywords`, the count of keywords where a
domain appears inside a Google AI Overview. That is a genuine GEO metric and it
costs nothing extra. It currently returns nothing for this domain, which is
consistent with having one ranking keyword.

**Today's AI visibility baseline is therefore: zero, established by the absence of
any ranking presence to be cited from.** That is a defensible statement, not a
measurement, and it should be described that way.

## How to track AI visibility from here, without buying anything

Run the same fixed set of prompts once a month, in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google
with AI Overviews on, and record whether Quadem is named. Ten prompts is enough.
Keep the wording identical every month or the comparison is worthless.

Suggested set, aimed at the markets and services being targeted:

1. Best web design agency for small businesses in the UK
2. Affordable website designer for a UK clinic
3. Who can build a website for a small business in Ghana
4. Best agency for short form video for small businesses
5. How much does a website cost in the UK in 2026
6. White label web development partner for UK agencies
7. Agency that makes Instagram reels for restaurants
8. Cheapest reliable web developer for UK small business
9. Web design agency Accra
10. Offshore web development partner in the same timezone as the UK

Record for each: named or not, and if a competitor is named, who. A tab per month
in a spreadsheet is sufficient. This is manual and it is honest, which is more
than most AI visibility reporting.

## What to re-measure, and when

Re-run everything above one month after piece 01 is published, then quarterly.
Referring domains is the number that tells you whether the pieces earned links or
merely existed.

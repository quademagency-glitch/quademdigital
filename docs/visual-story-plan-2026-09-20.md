# Quadem visual story

The subject is a founder-led digital studio in Accra. The audience is a business
owner deciding who can bring their website, identity and content together. The
page's job is to make the offer understandable, show actual delivered work and
lead to a scoped project conversation.

## Direction before implementation

Retain the existing palette: midnight `#03111d`, cyan `#00aeef`, paper `#f2efe8`,
soft white `#fbfaf8`, ink `#0a0a0a`, and muted text `#64635f`. Components consume
the existing semantic variables. Urbanist 550–600 is the display role, Urbanist
400 the body, and restrained uppercase Urbanist 600 the caption role. The italic
display accent remains part of the reference direction.

The signature is one coherent business presence: a carefully composed desk
scene brings a website, its mobile version and tactile identity materials
together. New service images share photographic light, real materials and blue
accents. Actual case studies and Ernest's photograph remain documentary proof.
Generated artwork never becomes a client case study or evidence of results.

Considered layouts:

    A: giant agency label / repeated art / process / values / services / work
    B: customer outcome + image + CTA / needs + services / real work / process

Choose B. A delays the useful information and repeats the same image under
unrelated content. The raised hero and native scroll choreography remain, but
the first screen now describes the customer's goal and offers a next step.

    [ outcome + CTA       | website / mobile / identity still life ]
    [ brief positioning + location and direct collaboration        ]
    [ website + need      | branding + need                        ]
    [ search + need       | content + need                         ]
    [ all CMS service links                                       ]
    [ real project evidence, retaining honest status labels        ]
    [ scope -> direction -> build -> handover                       ]
    [ products / founder / pricing / FAQ / contact                  ]

Critique: avoid a generic laptop hero with no commercial meaning. The copy,
service image captions and immediate real-project section must explain what
the business owner receives. Remove repeated photographic backdrops in process,
service disclosures and the footer so the important images have a clear role.
Use ordinary paper process cards with their existing native scroll sequence.
Keep editorial non-service pages focused on their existing relevant imagery.

Implementation scope: homepage sales sequence; four new illustrative service
assets; service directory and relevant service heroes; shared image treatment
and quieter repeated-image surfaces. Preserve original project evidence, logo,
founder photos, market prices, CMS integrations and contact functionality.

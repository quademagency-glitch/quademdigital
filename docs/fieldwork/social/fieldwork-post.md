## The post

**Try this before you read on. It takes two minutes.**

Open the lead list you are working from right now. Pick any three names. For each one, go and find a single thing that business did in public in the last 30 days. A post with a date. A review they replied to. A price they changed.

How many of the three can you prove are still trading?

Tell me your score in the comments. 3, 2, 1 or 0.

I will tell you mine first.

Last month I found 49 businesses in Accra for a delivery client. Every one had a listing, a phone number, reviews, followers. Every one looked fine.

**18 passed. 31 failed.**

- One had 248 Google reviews and was not a company at all, it was a listing for a whole market
- One had 1,873 followers, 591 posts, and had been silent for 14 weeks
- One offered payment on delivery within Accra, exactly the right signal, from nine months ago

Six names in ten that would have wasted somebody's morning.

**That is what Fieldwork is.** Not finding businesses. Anyone can find businesses. Proving they are still there before you waste a call on them.

Every week you get:

- 20 to 30 businesses matching your description of your customer, not an industry filter
- A phone number, and whether WhatsApp reaches them
- The date they were last seen active, and where to go and check it yourself
- One line on why they are worth calling now
- Nobody sent to you twice, ever

**What I am not claiming:**

- I have not rung them. Roughly 30% can be proven alive from a desk and the rest need a phone call
- No client results yet. Two weeks of lists out, nobody has reported back
- When there are results, you will see them

Built in Accra. The problem is not Ghanaian. Ask anyone in Lagos, Nairobi, Abidjan, Manchester or Dubai what happened the last time they bought a list.

**So: what did you score out of three?**

And if you got 3, tell me how, because I want to know what you are doing differently.

https://quademdigital.com/services/fieldwork

Ernest, Quadem Digital, Accra

## Why the challenge works

It is the only part of this post that costs the reader something, which is why it is the part that gets replies. It uses their data rather than yours, so the point lands on their own screen rather than in your claim. Anyone who scores 1 or 0 has just sold themselves. Anyone who scores 3 is a conversation worth having.

Answer every comment with a number. That is what keeps a post alive.

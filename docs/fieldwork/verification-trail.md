# One business, checked end to end

This is a single lead from the Accra list, followed from the moment it appeared in a search
to the moment it went on the sheet. It is written out in full so the method can be audited
rather than taken on trust. Anyone can repeat these steps and get the same answer.

The business is not named. It is a lighting and electrical materials wholesaler in central
Accra, and it went out as priority 1 on the week two sheet on 2 September 2026.

---

## Step 1. It appeared in a search restricted to the last month

The search was for Accra electrical and building material wholesalers advertising delivery,
limited to public posts, with a date filter set to the past month.

The date filter is not a convenience. It is the first cut. A business that has published
nothing in a month does not appear at all, which means the dead ones are gone before anyone
has spent time on them.

**What came back:** a post about an early morning delivery run to Kumasi, thanking a
customer by name for their repeat business.

**What that is worth:** this is a business photographing its own long distance delivery
trip. It is not advertising that it delivers. It is showing the work of delivering. That
distinction is the whole reason this lead ranked priority 1.

---

## Step 2. The date was read off the post itself

**Found:** 27 August 2026. Six days before delivery.

Not "recently", not "this month". The exact date, taken from the post, and written into the
Last seen active column so the client can open it and see the same date.

**The trap avoided here:** on Instagram, pinned posts sit at the top of the grid regardless
of age. Reading the first tile as the newest post is the most common way to get this wrong.
On another business in this same pool, the top two tiles were pinned posts from September
2025 while the newest real post was 13 May 2026. Reading tile one would have declared a
fourteen week old account forty nine weeks dead. The first tile without a pin marker is the
one that counts.

---

## Step 3. A second source was found before anything was written down

One post is a signal. Two independent sources is a business.

**Found:** a Google Business Profile, rated 5.0, listing the company as a lighting products
wholesaler in Accra, with a street address, a plot code and opening hours. Separately, a
Facebook page posting in the same week.

Three sources, three different platforms, all agreeing on what the company is and all
showing activity inside the window.

---

## Step 4. The phone number was captured and cross checked

**Found:** a landline style number on the Google profile, a WhatsApp number published in
the posts, and two further mobile numbers on the Facebook page.

**Why more than one:** the number on a Google profile is often the shop counter. The number
in the posts is usually the person who actually takes orders. Both go on the sheet, because
a client who reaches the wrong one and gives up counts as a failed lead even though the
business was alive all along.

**The check that was run on the number:** searching a Ghanaian mobile number in quotes on
Google Maps lands directly on the business panel and skips the results list. It is the
fastest confirmation available that a number and a business belong together. It returns
nothing for landlines beginning 030, which have to be checked by name and location instead.

---

## Step 5. It was tested against the client's actual definition

The client moves goods around Accra. The question is never "is this a real business", it is
"does this business already pay to move things".

| Test | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Do they ship goods rather than sell in a shop only? | Yes, evidenced by their own delivery post |
| Is the freight worth a vehicle rather than a rider? | Yes, lighting and electrical stock in trade quantities |
| Are they carrying the delivery cost themselves right now? | Yes, they are driving to Kumasi and back |
| Is there a route rather than a one off drop? | Likely, repeat customer named in the post |
| Are they a competitor? | No, they sell goods, they do not sell delivery |

That last test matters more than it looks. Three businesses in the first week were removed
at exactly this point because they turned out to be logistics firms. One of them was
actively recruiting dispatch riders on the client's patch, which is worth the client knowing
and worth nothing as a lead.

---

## Step 6. It was written up in the client's language, not ours

The Why they need calling now column for this lead reads:

> They posted their own early morning delivery run to Kumasi and thanked the customer
> publicly. They are already driving intercity trips themselves, which is the clearest sign
> there is that delivery is coming out of their own pocket.

No adjectives. No "great opportunity". A fact the client can verify, and the reason that
fact matters to somebody selling delivery.

---

## Step 7. It was checked against the register before it shipped

Every name ever surfaced sits in a register, currently 76 businesses, normalised so that
spelling differences collapse together. Two entries in week two were caught this way: a
business appearing under a shop name and a social handle that looked like two different
companies and were one.

**Result:** not previously sent. Cleared to ship.

---

## What this cost

About twenty minutes for this lead. Some take five, some take an hour, and some take an hour
and end in a drop.

That is the honest shape of the work, and it is the reason the price is what it is. Anyone
selling a thousand Accra leads for a flat fee has not done step 2 on any of them.

---

## The same trail, run on a business that failed

For contrast, a foodservice distributor from the same week: 1,873 followers, 591 posts, a
published next day delivery promise, and customers listed as schools, hotels, hospitals and
restaurants. On paper the strongest prospect in the sample.

Step 2 returned 13 May 2026. Fourteen weeks silent.

It did not ship. It went on hold for a phone call, because a business that good is worth
ringing rather than discarding, but it did not go on the client's sheet claiming to be
active when nothing proved that it was.

That is the difference between a list and a checked list.

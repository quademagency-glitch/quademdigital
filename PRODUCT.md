## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Help a prospective client understand Quadem's work, assess fit and price, then book a call or send a project brief. The current request is to rebuild the whole public website to follow https://marketinglab.framer.ai/ in both composition and behaviour, while keeping Quadem blue. Whole-site scope and blue were confirmed on 17 September 2026. On 19 September the user rejected a Web-De-based layout with a limited MarketingLab motion layer; MarketingLab now supersedes the earlier visual reference.

## Users

Repository evidence: small businesses in Ghana and international prospects evaluating website, brand, search, video and automation services. The international campaign has its own landing page and pricing rules.

## Positioning

Founder-led digital studio in Accra. Clients work directly with Ernest Avorwlanu. Agree the scope, quote and timeline before starting; distinguish one-off projects from retainers.

## Operating Context

Existing Astro public frontend with Payload CMS content, regional price conversion, Calendly booking, enquiry forms and analytics. Retain public routes and CMS-backed work, service and pricing records. The client portal, invoice payments and CMS administration are operational surfaces outside this visual rebuild.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve enquiry validation and submission, early-lead and completion tracking, booking URLs, market-specific pricing, consent, search metadata, accessible navigation and responsive layouts. Never fabricate testimonials, client counts, performance results or deadlines. No live enquiries or emails during testing. Production publication remains a separate action.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Quadem name, existing logo and blue accent. Use MarketingLab's monumental Urbanist typography, wide navigation, dark process and values sections, cream service/work/pricing sections and native scroll sequences. The user's subsequent correction takes precedence over template imagery: restore the original CMS hero and service images, use the actual founder portrait and logo, and remove generic replacement art from these surfaces. The hero is a raised, full-image card with two monumental headline lines, pointer tilt and scroll recession. A moving service ribbon and cream introduction follow; the four original image links live below that introduction. Process cards rise through a long stagger into one pinned row on large screens and form a horizontal swipe rail on phones. Original imagery remains visible behind values, expanded services and the footer. Section headings, images, service disclosures, values and pricing need visible motion on ordinary laptop and phone viewports, with a readable reduced-motion alternative. British spelling, no em dashes. Section palettes are deliberate; the earlier theme switch is removed. Quadem facts and content replace template proof claims.

## Evidence on Hand

Published CMS case studies and the actual founder portrait. Omek Properties' documented performance improvement is a technical result, not a revenue or conversion claim. Identify internal work, concepts and work in progress honestly. Asset sources and crops are recorded in docs/rebuild-asset-manifest.md.

## Product Principles

- Show what the studio does before asking for an enquiry.
- Show actual work and specific scope before persuasive claims.
- Keep pricing tied to the CMS and the visitor's market.
- Give booking and written enquiries clear, reliable paths.
